# Casa del Espacio — Image Schedule & Asset Brief
Rev A · what to send, and in what form.

Every image slot on the site is specified below. Send the highest-resolution
originals you have; do not pre-crop, pre-compress, or pre-grade — the build
pipeline handles all three, and cropping early destroys the focal-point
freedom the layouts need.

---

## Global capture / selection notes

- **Colour temperature** — warm, 3200–4000K. If a shot is neutral or cool,
  say so and I'll grade it; don't correct it yourself.
- **Shadows** — keep them lifted (roughly 8–12% floor). The site darkens
  images further under text overlays; anything already crushed goes muddy.
- **Highlights** — protect the brass/tungsten highlights. Blown pendant
  lights are the one place the identity lives, and they cannot be recovered.
- **Greens** — plants should read desaturated and olive, not vivid.
- **People** — welcome, but at Puli register: motion-blurred, incidental,
  seen from behind or in passing. Never posed, never looking at camera.
- **Verticals** — keep architectural verticals straight. Converging lines
  read as amateur at the sizes we're displaying.
- **No** wide-angle distortion, no HDR halos, no drone hero shots.

## Per-project data (needed alongside the images)

The Villa Lumière metadata pattern is load-bearing — it's what makes a
project page read as architecture rather than a listing. For each project:

- Project name
- Location (city / region) and country
- Latitude / longitude, to 4 decimal places
- Year completed
- Typology (residence, hospitality, retail…)
- Gross floor area, m²
- Status (completed / in progress / concept)
- Lead architect and/or interior designer, with credit spelling as they want it
- Photographer credit
- 2–4 sentences of description, in Casa's voice
- Spec-table rows: any figures worth publishing (units, materials, site area,
  ceiling height, completion date)

Placeholder text here will make the layout lie to us. Rough and real beats
polished and invented.
